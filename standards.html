Standards & Files

The rulebook — short, because the team is small.

How we work

  • Check every file before printing. No exceptions.
  • Prices come from the sheets. Nobody invents or changes a price.
  • Quality before speed. A bad sign reprinted is slower than a check done right.
  • Write it down here. If a way of doing something changes, this portal changes the same day — that is how we stay franchise-ready.

Where files live

Working files, price sheets and company documents live in the Signs2Go Drive folders (the Franchise Operations library). This portal links to them and explains them — it does not store sensitive documents, and passwords never go on this site.

About this portal

Built for Signs2Go's two-person team: the manager and the printer operator. Run by Bobby. To change something, tell Bobby — updates publish here the same way, in S2G black, white and green.

Signs2Go Portal — the internal team site. Team use only. No passwords or sensitive documents live here.
